### Hi there! I'm David

I'm a machine learning engineer at TomTom. I build large-scale perception and mapping systems and the MLOps pipelines behind them. My models detect traffic signs and traffic lights across Europe and North America.

- **Background:** computer vision research. I did a research master's in AI at the University of Amsterdam and wrote my thesis on partially supervised instance segmentation, which became a first-author paper at ICCV 2021 ([OPMask](https://github.com/dbtmpl/OPMask)).
- **Day to day:** the whole ML lifecycle, from data ingestion and ground truth to training, distributed inference, evaluation and release. Lately I build coding agents that run our core processes locally and in ephemeral environments, check their results in GIS sessions, and iterate on problems on their own.
- **Interested in:** turning new ML research into dependable products, especially in perception and generative modelling. Also efficient computer vision on edge devices and physics-based machine learning, currently diffusion models seen through statistical physics: rare events, transition paths, and the thermodynamics of learning.
